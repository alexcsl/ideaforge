The Council

critiques, peer review, verdict

Response A (The Contrarian)

The payout tool dies if the marketplace closes its API. Build the parser to tolerate scraping fallback or it is one policy change from zero.

Response B (The Market Realist)

Reconciliation has clear willingness to pay. Sellers already lose billable hours. Returns analytics is softer; sellers say they want it but rarely pay for visibility alone.

Response C (The Executor)

Reconciliation MVP is a weekend of parsing plus a table view. Returns clustering needs data volume most small sellers do not have on day one.

Peer-review consensus

Reviewers converged on the payout reconciliation idea as the strongest on demand and buildability. The returns idea ranked second but carried a data-availability risk. The support-macro idea was judged the weakest on evidence.

Chairman verdict

Lead with payout reconciliation. It has the clearest observed pain, the shortest path to a usable version, and direct willingness to pay. Treat the marketplace API as a dependency risk and design a scraping fallback from day one. Returns analytics is a credible second once a seller relationship exists. The support-macro idea should not be built before five validation interviews.
